Proto-Indo-European (PIE) is a language that linguists have reconstructed to be the parent language of all the Indo-European languages. English and Sanskrit are also Indo-European languages.

> Asterisks mean that the word is reconstructed, not documented; dashes mean that the word is not complete, but rather just a stem; “th” is a single sound, like in English; any diacritics imply a different, unrelated sound.

Below is a chart that shows a few words in Proto-Indo-European and their descendants in English and Sanskrit.
