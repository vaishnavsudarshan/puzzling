Uzbek is a language spoken natively by over 34 million people in Uzbekistan, as well as in parts of nearby countries like Afghanistan, Tajikistan, and China.

You get hired to work at a questionable pet store in Uzbekistan, but you mix up the signs of all the animals, and you don't know any Uzbek!

To avoid getting fired, determine the correct correspondences.

## Questions

### Match each Uzbek phrase (left) to its English meaning (right).
- yovuz mushuk => evil cat
- mushuk yo'q => no cat
- maymun va sincap => monkey and squirrel
- ko'k sincap => blue squirrel
- yovuz it => evil dog
- non va mushuk => bread and cat
- och ko'k mushuk => light blue cat
- kulgili quyon => funny rabbit
- och yashil sarig'i => light green yolk
- sincaplar => squirrels
- bo'ri va it => wolf and dog
- kulgili odam => funny human
