Neural networks stack layers that each apply weights and biases, then an activation function.

## Questions

### In a few sentences, explain why a neural network needs nonlinear activation functions — and what happens without them.
type: ai
grade: |
  Full credit if the student explains that without nonlinear activations, stacking linear
  layers collapses into a single linear (affine) transformation, so the network can only
  represent linear relationships no matter how many layers it has — and that nonlinearity is
  what lets it approximate complex, non-linear functions. Give partial credit for clearly
  stating just one of those two ideas. Be lenient about wording and notation. Give little or
  no credit for answers that are off-topic or just restate the question without explanation.
